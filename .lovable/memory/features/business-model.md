---
name: Business model
description: FurnishedFinder-style marketplace with cheaper landlord subscriptions and free tenant screening
type: feature
---
- Landlord monthly: $9.99/mo
- Landlord annual: $99/yr (best value)
- Tenants: completely free
- Tenant screening: free (competitive advantage vs FF's $44.99)
- No commissions on any rental
- Minimum 30-day furnished stays
- Focus: Fresno County, CA (Fresno, Clovis, Madera, Sanger, Selma, Reedley)
- Target: traveling nurses, therapists, contract healthcare workers relocating to Central Valley
